Title: Incident Response Standard Operating Procedure
Effective Date: 2024-02-10
Owner: Security Operations

1. Purpose
This SOP describes how to classify, escalate, and close security and availability incidents.

2. Severity Levels
2.1 SEV-1: Active data breach, ransomware, or total outage of a production regulated system.
2.2 SEV-2: Partial outage or suspected unauthorized access with no confirmed exfiltration.
2.3 SEV-3: Limited impact, workaround available; monitor and schedule fix.
2.4 SEV-4: Informational finding or policy exception request.

3. Initial Response
3.1 Acknowledge SEV-1 within 15 minutes of detection.
3.2 Open an incident channel and appoint an incident commander.
3.3 Preserve evidence; do not wipe hosts before forensics approval.

4. Escalation Contacts
4.1 SEV-1: page Security On-Call and notify the Compliance Lead within 30 minutes.
4.2 SEV-2: Security On-Call within 1 hour; Compliance if customer data may be involved.
4.3 External breach notifications follow Legal guidance; engineering must not notify customers directly.

5. Closure
5.1 Every SEV-1 and SEV-2 requires a written post-incident review within 5 business days.
5.2 Action items must have an owner and due date before the incident is marked closed.
