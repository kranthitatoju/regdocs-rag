Title: Access Control Policy
Effective Date: 2024-01-15
Owner: Information Security

1. Purpose
This policy defines authentication and authorization requirements for systems that process regulated product information.

2. Password Requirements
2.1 Passwords must be at least 14 characters and include upper, lower, numeric, and symbol characters.
2.2 Password reuse of the last 24 passwords is prohibited.
2.3 Temporary passwords must expire within 24 hours and force a change on first login.

3. Multi-Factor Authentication
3.1 MFA is required for all remote access and for privileged (admin) accounts.
3.2 Acceptable second factors: authenticator app (TOTP) or hardware security key.
3.3 SMS OTP is not permitted for privileged accounts.

4. Account Lockout
4.1 Accounts lock after 5 consecutive failed login attempts within 15 minutes.
4.2 Lockout duration is 30 minutes, or until an administrator unlocks the account.
4.3 All lockout events must be logged and retained per the Data Retention Policy.

5. Access Reviews
5.1 Privileged access is reviewed quarterly.
5.2 Terminated user access must be revoked within 4 hours of HR notification.
