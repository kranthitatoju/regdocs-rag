Title: Data Retention Policy
Effective Date: 2024-03-01
Owner: Privacy & Compliance

1. Purpose
This policy defines how long operational and personal data are retained for systems supporting regulated workflows.

2. Application Logs
2.1 Security and access logs are retained for 365 days online.
2.2 After 365 days, logs are archived for an additional 2 years, then securely deleted.
2.3 Log deletion jobs must produce an audit record including count of records removed.

3. Personal Data (PII)
3.1 Customer PII used for support tickets is retained for 90 days after ticket closure.
3.2 Employee PII in HR systems follows local legal minimums; default is 7 years after termination when no longer required.
3.3 PII must not be copied into unstructured scratch folders without an approved retention tag.

4. Model and Embedding Artifacts
4.1 Derived embeddings of regulated documents inherit the retention of the source document.
4.2 Evaluation run outputs are retained for 180 days unless marked as a release baseline.

5. Legal Hold
5.1 Legal hold overrides scheduled deletion until counsel releases the hold in writing.
