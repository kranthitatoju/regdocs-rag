Title: Vendor Risk Checklist
Effective Date: 2024-04-01
Owner: Third-Party Risk

1. Purpose
Use this checklist before onboarding a vendor that will process or store regulated or personal data.

2. Security Questionnaire
2.1 Confirm SOC 2 Type II (or equivalent) report dated within the last 12 months.
2.2 Confirm encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest for stored customer data.
2.3 Confirm MFA for vendor employee access to production systems.

3. Data Handling
3.1 Document data categories shared with the vendor (e.g., PII, clinical, commercial).
3.2 Require a data processing agreement before any production data transfer.
3.3 Confirm subprocessors are listed and change-notification terms exist.

4. Exit and Continuity
4.1 Require data return or certified destruction within 30 days of contract end.
4.2 Confirm RTO/RPO targets if the vendor is on a critical path for regulated operations.
4.3 Store the completed checklist under the vendor record in the procurement system.

5. Residual Risk
5.1 High residual risk requires Director-level approval before go-live.
5.2 Reassess critical vendors at least annually or after a material security incident.
